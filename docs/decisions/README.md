# Decisions

Architecture Decision Records in [MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/) format. New records start from [template.md](template.md). Rules: [documentation guidelines](../guidelines/documentation.md#architecture-decision-records).

## Areas

Every ADR has one area, the part of the app or the work it is mainly about; `pnpm adr <area>` lists them. The areas of the app are named after its modules in `apps/web/src/lib`.

- `artworks`: adding, editing and showing one artwork, its form, own fields and the archive
- `images`: artwork images: sources, uploads, thumbnails, caching and the loupe
- `pins`: pins on the artwork image
- `wall`: the Atelier wall: order, grouping, filters and loading
- `card-index`: Kartoteka: its lists, tabs and own lists
- `values`: category values, their pages, details and icons
- `notes`: artwork notes and links in notes
- `glossary`: Słowniczek: terms, definitions and their forms
- `timeline`: Kalendarium: the timeline and historical events
- `chronicle`: Kronika: museum visits
- `review`: Powtórki: sessions, questions, scheduling and the streak
- `career`: Ekspozycja: points, ranks, exhibits and their engravings
- `exhibitions`: Wystawy: exhibitions and their editor
- `statistics`: Statystyki: statistics of the collection
- `compare`: Porównaj and the palette on the stage
- `profile`: Ustawienia: the profile, preferences and the settings view
- `auth`: signing up and in, passwords, auth emails and account deletion
- `data-transfer`: export and import of the atelier
- `legal`: the public start page, the terms and the privacy policy
- `feedback`: feedback from learners
- `shell`: the menu, the top bar, navigation, the theme and loading
- `process`: how work flows: decisions, git, tracking and tooling
- `architecture`: the stack, hosting, deployment, monitoring, code layout, caching and tests
- `database`: the data model, migrations, environments, limits and backups
- `design-system`: design tokens, the UI library and shared view patterns
- `texts`: UI texts and how they are kept
